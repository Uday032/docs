## What is Svalync?
Svalync is a no-code AI platform that helps teams automate boring, repetitive tasks. Imagine handing off phone calls, customer chats, or data entry to smart AI agents—so you can focus on what you love (like growing your business).

Built for: Non-technical teams in small businesses, education, travel, sales, or any growing company.
How it works: Connect your tools (like email or CRM), build simple workflows with drag-and-drop, and let AI do the heavy lifting.
Big wins: Automate up to 90% of repetitive work, save 50+ hours a week, and respond to customers 24/7.

No tech skills needed—just point, click, and relax!
